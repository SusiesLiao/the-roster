# Velaire / Parti / Roster Suite — brand palette

Version 1.0 · Approved by Susan Liao · 27 September 2026

## Direction

Quiet, editorial and architectural. Powder blue, deep blue-grey ink and soft ivory, supported by pale mist, blue and sage-grey surfaces. Low saturation throughout. Fashion references describe the mood; these are our recorded colours, not official fashion-house colour specifications.

## How to use the palette

- Keep most of the composition light: ivory canvas, powder-blue fields and airy pale panels.
- Set headings and strong actions in deep blue-grey ink. Use slate blue sparingly for links, labels and emphasis.
- The pale mist, blue and sage-grey cards can sit together, as in Susan’s approved reference.
- Keep Velaire, Parti and Roster Suite in one shared family. Distinguish their roles through naming, typography and content.
- Do not reintroduce plum, mauve, burgundy or saturated crimson. The burgundy mockup was an alternative, not the approved palette.
- Colour must not be the only indicator of a state or action. Use text, underlines, icons or other clear cues as appropriate.

## Typography and imagery context

Elegant editorial serif headlines, restrained sans-serif UI and body text, generous whitespace and fine rules. Keep suitable Chinese serif and sans-serif fallbacks for 简体 and 繁體. Use the selected living-room photo for the hero and the spiral staircase for the lower founder section; keep their natural warmth.

## Implementation status

This records the approved palette. It does not itself recolour the current preview or deploy production. The current preview already uses powder blue #DFE5EC; other existing preview tokens may differ and should be aligned in the next styling pass.

## Brand language

Velaire is the parent company and advisory/implementation business. Parti is the system of record. Roster is the system of action. The public product environment is always **Roster Suite**. Existing legacy internal schema names stay unchanged.
