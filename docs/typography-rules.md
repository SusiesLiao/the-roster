# Velaire heading rules

Use these rules across English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese unless Susan specifies an exception.

- Headings have no periods.
- Use one line where space allows; wrap naturally instead of inserting arbitrary breaks.
- Keep the product-offer name “Operating Diagnostic” together on one line. The preceding “The Velaire” may appear on its own line. Scale the phrase to fit smaller screens.
- The English closing separates “Connect the relationships” and “Let the business thrive” onto two lines.
- The English Advisory fit heading uses three lines: “The work is strong”, “But running the business”, “feels harder than it should”.
